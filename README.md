# HiveMesh — From Hive to Consumer

### Blockchain-Based Honey Traceability & Smart Beekeeping Management Platform

| Smart India Hackathon 2026 | SIH26021 |

## Project Vision

HiveMesh is a blockchain-based platform designed to create a
digital and traceable record for every honey batch, covering its
journey from the hive to the consumer.

HiveMesh provides a digital traceability system that records
the journey of a honey batch across:

**Hive → Harvest → Processing → Packaging → Distribution → Consumer**

The platform combines blockchain traceability, smart beekeeping
management and QR-based batch tracking.

## present scenario

The honey supply chain faces challenges such as:

- Limited monitoring of hive conditions
- Incomplete hive and harvest records
- Multiple intermediaries
- Difficulty tracing honey back to its source
- Dependence on manual records
- Limited visibility for consumers


## essential elements
